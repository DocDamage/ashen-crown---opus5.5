The Ashen Crown - third-party art credits
=========================================
Library art is assembled at build time into game/assets/ext/ by tools/gen_art.py library and ships inside the
game only; the raw files are not redistributed. Per-asset sources and licences: reports/asset_ledger.json.

[Time Fantasy - finalbossblues / Jason Perry - https://timefantasy.net]
  Used under the owner's Time Fantasy licence; credit finalbossblues.
  - Field tilesets: Steampunk, Sewers, Ruins, Ashlands, Winter, Fairy Forest, Cloud City, Atlantis,
    Future Fantasy (tiles recoloured and assembled into autotile atlases by tools/art/tiles.py)
  - Field props: Winter tents and signposts, Ruins statues and pillars, Cloud City angel statues and bells,
    Fairy Forest obelisks, Future Fantasy awnings, Ashlands signposts; rails, laundry lines and world-map
    mountains are composed by tools/art/tiles.py from Steampunk, Future Fantasy and Fairy Forest pixels
  - "Elements" character kit: hero and NPC field sprites and battle sprites
    (pieces recoloured and assembled by tools/art/world_sprites.py and tools/art/battle.py)
  - Beast Tribes: Dain's field sprite, emotes and side-view battler (recoloured)
  - NPC animations: child, elder, guard, farmer, baker, monk field sprites
  - Future Fantasy treasure chests

[World map and extra props]
  - Winlu Fantasy Tileset - Overworld by WinLu (https://winlu.itch.io/fantasy-overworld), "Other Engines"
    edition: mountains, settlements, dungeon/ruin/gate markers, rowboats. Commercial use allowed; reduced to 16px.
  - CraftPix.net free 2D top-down pixel dungeon asset pack: braziers, ladders (https://craftpix.net/file-licenses/).
  - SakPix stage assets (https://sakpix.itch.io): altar and hand-cart, reduced to game scale.

[Battle: enemies and backdrops]
  - Ansimuz Legacy Collection by ansimuz (https://ansimuz.itch.io/gothicvania-patreon-collection)
    TinyRPG battle monsters, Gothicvania / Warped characters and parallax environments.
    Commercial use confirmed by the creator on the itch page. Credit: ansimuz.
  - Monster sprite packs (Stone Golem, Huge Knight, Headless Horseman, Cerberus, Gargoyle, Gryphon, Imp, Witch)
    from the owner's "characters" library folder; licence shipped in each pack (License.txt): use in any game
    project, personal or commercial; do not resell/redistribute as a game asset; no NFTs.
  - Factory Monster Pack 1 by Haydeos (https://haydeos.itch.io/factory-monster-pack-1)
    "You may use these assets in any commercial or non-commercial game project"; any engine.
  All library battlers/backdrops were modified (recoloured, re-edged, relit, recomposed) for this game.

Portraits, fallback sprites and tiles, UI, fonts and audio are generated by the project's own code (tools/).
