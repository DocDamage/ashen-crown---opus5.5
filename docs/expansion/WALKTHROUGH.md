# The Ashen Crown: walkthrough

This is a spoiler guide to the whole game: the main story chapter by chapter, where the expansion content opens, every questline, the optional bosses and superbosses, and the post-game. Hero names follow the release cast. Tips assume Normal difficulty.

## Before you start

- **Saving.** Save at save points or with a portable save point. Quicksave works on the world map, and the game autosaves on map changes. Before the Final Descent, the game writes a protected backup.
- **Parties.** Up to five heroes fight at once. Three more wait in reserve and share part of the XP. Change the formation (and rows) from the menu, anywhere outside battle.
- **Bonds.** Heroes who win battles together grow closer (Records > Bonds). Every bond level with a partner in the party adds 1% attack and defence, up to 8%. At bond 2 and bond 4 some pairs have a short talk the next time you rest at an inn.
- **The world.** The world map uses Mode-7 and tilts with your vehicle. Open the minimap and full map with M (or L3). Quest markers show on both. The day/night cycle lasts about 20 minutes, and some NPCs and encounters only appear at night.
- **Level breaks.** The level cap is 99. The first ancient dragon raises it to 120, all four raise it to 150, and the Unmade Crown raises it to 200. Damage can go past 9,999 once the caps rise.

## Main story
### CH01: The Orders We Carry
Raven escorts a ministry inspection into Crown Quarry. The workers have been told that a delayed shift is sabotage; their lift is flooding while officials argue about who may stop production. Morwen, a detained relay apprentice, can hold one pump open but not both. Raven must learn movement, interaction, defending and magic while choosing the order in which to open rescue routes. Every worker is recoverable; there is no hidden timer or reward for abandoning people.
*Turning point:* The extractor speaks Raven's childhood nickname in a voice he has never heard. A guard calls the sound a pressure artifact. After the battle, the voice says only: “That is not a stone.” Raven refuses the restart order. Morwen takes the worker list, not the valuable heartglass. Mara brings the rescued crew out through the lift yard.
### CH02: Orders in Ashes
Veyr celebrates the inspection as a successful reopening. Raven tries to correct the record and is arrested. Aurex, summoned to certify Morwen's mental fitness, recognizes that the charge sheet was written before the inspection. She opens a service door but insists they take the records rather than simply flee. The escape introduces switches, readable patrol movement and a nonlethal visual presentation for human opponents.
*Turning point:* In the ledger stacks, Raven finds living workers listed as expendable material. Aurex finds her own signature on an older authorization. Defeating the Brass Bailiff opens the canal; it does not burn down the entire capital or turn all guards into villains. Clerk Ansel quietly leaves the gate unlatched.
### CH03: A Road Without Banners
Elowen is guiding families around a closed military road. She initially refuses Raven because his unit escorted the convoys that displaced them. A damaged irrigation network is starving one camp and flooding another. The party repairs the route using visible sluices, learning that a convenient shortcut can shift danger onto somebody else.
*Turning point:* The Rootbound Stag is attacking the valves because extraction residue is reaching its grove. Victory breaks a coercive growth seal. Elowen agrees to guide the party after Raven helps move the camp without asking forgiveness in return. A quiet meal gives each current member a small scene rather than more exposition.
### CH04: The Walking Furnace
Cinderwake is not a villain's factory but a town that will freeze if its machines simply stop. the Golem shows the party a regulator that converts pressure into steadier power. Morwen recognizes it as the mechanism used to amplify the voices in the quarry. When a regulator walks out of its housing and begins destroying the workshop, the player must vent pressure away from occupied rooms.
*Turning point:* the Golem admits the regulator is his design. His husband Pell asks him to solve the heating problem before becoming a traveling revolutionary. After the Colossus falls, the crew rigs a temporary waterwheel with the workers. The repair is imperfect and visibly limited. the Golem joins to learn what the heartglass actually contains.
### CH05: Paper Ghosts
Bellharbor's officials are hunting a forger who has been turning refugees into cargo. Sak offers passage in exchange for recovering a confiscated ledger. This is a town infiltration sequence built from dialogue choices, route observation and one short chase with local retries; failure does not erase progress or cause a story dead end.
*Turning point:* The ledger reveals that Sak marks people dead to get them through checkpoints. Jori, his brother, says the rescued families now cannot claim wages or housing. Sak laughs until one of those families asks which name they are allowed to use. He takes the party to the drowned archive to establish a record outside the ministry.
### CH06: The Bell That Broke
The archive holds contracts from before the kingdom consolidated the extraction industry. Three bell tones open testimony galleries. Elian Rook, a former ministry geologist, arrives with evidence that the “fossil” organs are still transmitting a distributed consciousness. He offers names, dates and physical records rather than demanding faith.
*Turning point:* The Custodian tries to seal the archive against unauthorized readers. After the battle, Rook asks Raven to bring him to the last synchronization chamber. Raven agrees only to investigate. Rook conceals that he has already placed a second activation route. A bell rings above the water as the town preserves copies of the evidence.
### CH07: To Borrow Sky
The route to the Pale Basin crosses the Skychain. Vespera offers a dramatic solo leap to reset its anchors. Elowen points out that there is room for an ordinary maintenance crew. The dungeon gives the player safe ballast controls and wind cues rather than asking for a mandatory precision platforming skill.
*Turning point:* The Chain Roc has been tethered as a living stabilizer. Vespera breaks the tether while the crew secures an alternate bridge, surviving the act he expected to turn into a memorial. He does not yet know how to accept an achievement that did not nearly kill him.

**The Deep opens (CH07b).** After the Skychain, a courier at the Bellharbor quay brings Mara's letter: the Crown Quarry floor has collapsed onto something below. Return to the Heartglass Face (Crown Quarry, lower gallery). The new east passage opens only after the letter. Follow the Crown survey stakes down *The Collapse* to the Breach, which leads into the Deep's first realm, Emberdeep. This starts **QDEEP, The Quarry Below** (boss: Foreman Grisk's Excavator). The Deep has three realms: Emberdeep (the delver kingdom), the Lattice (the builder cities, run by the sleeping mind CURATOR), and the Hollow Throne (the realm of the dead). Each opens further as the story advances.
### CH08: Traitor's Edge
Oni intercepts the party at Whitebone Redoubt and offers access to its prisoners in exchange for safe conduct. Raven distrusts her because she wrote the binding warrants. Aurex refuses a deal that merely replaces one group of prisoners with another. The party enters to obtain each captive's actual release key, giving the names hall a practical role.
*Turning point:* The Adjudicator attempts to transfer the ward to Raven. Oni destroys her officer's seal rather than use it to control him. It is a useful act, not forgiveness. A freed prisoner tells her where to send her testimony. Oni joins with the explicit understanding that she will return to answer for what she did.
### CH09: A Voice with My Hands
The Memory Vault separates three histories: an ancient voluntary shared-body pact; its industrial standardization; and the later confinement of dragon consciousness across engineered lineages. Dragonborn are people, not counterfeit descendants waiting to be replaced. Raven can decline an invasive memory projection; documentary evidence supplies the same required information.
*Turning point:* The Pale Choir tries to impose one agreed history on contradictory accounts. The battle breaks that forced synchronization. Ilyr, the fragment in Raven, first asks to use Raven's body to rebuild his own. Raven answers that wanting freedom does not make another body empty. Ilyr agrees to speak without taking control, a limited first accord.
### CH10: The Smaller Accord
The party returns evidence to three communities. Each wants a different immediate guarantee: Cinderwake needs heat, High Aerie needs stable transit, and Nacre needs a way to separate consent from institutional pressure. These are compact authored conversations with one local intervention each, not three identical crystal fetch quests.
*Turning point:* the Golem and Aurex demonstrate a slow, local release that keeps one relay alive without coercion. It works, but needs coordinated labor and time. Rook calls it an intolerable delay while the extraction continues. Morwen almost agrees with him before a volunteer describes the cost of being forced to be rescued.
### CH11: Below the Coronation
Voss is preparing to capture the network and replace unstable individual relays with one central authority. The party reroutes occupied lifts and disables the visible synchronization lines. A clear warning marks the last freely explorable pre-catastrophe moment; the game writes a separate protected transition backup without overwriting manual saves.
*Turning point:* Voss fights on the Marshal Bridge using commands that turn party protection against itself. He retreats into the central apparatus when defeated. The party wins this encounter normally. The evacuation actions determine practical aftermath details, not whether the predetermined catastrophe happens.
### CH12: Crown of Cinders
Rook insists that a century of exploitation cannot be repaired one volunteer at a time. The party defeats him and disconnects the Crown Dais. His hidden synchronization route, previously foreshadowed through mismatched diagrams and a missing relay on the Golem's plan, completes the activation anyway. The game does not fake a winnable boss or ask for an impossible final input.
*Turning point:* Ilyr refuses Rook's order to overwrite Raven. Aurex breaks the common command channel while the Golem diverts power to the evacuation lifts. These actions reduce the disaster but cannot undo it. Coastlines fracture along old relay faults, dormant dragon structures rise, and the party is scattered. The escape is an authored sequence with local retries, followed by silence rather than a victory fanfare.

**The rescue at the lift (the Shadow scene).** At the end of the collapsing aqueduct, you learn the relay tenders are still chained in the cells below. Pick **five** of Morwen, Vespera, the Golem, Elowen, Oni, Sak, Kitsune and Archangel to go down. Everyone else holds the lift cage.

- **The timer.** A hidden timer starts. The cable lasts about 100 seconds of field time; the clock pauses during dialogue.
- **Arrivals.** The rescuers climb back one at a time, at about 32, 50, 66, 81 and 96 seconds.
- **Hints.** Ilyr, Aurex and a tender tell you to wait, and the shaking gets worse as time runs down.
- **The best outcome.** Wait for all five. The last one leaps the gap at the last second and the cage drops by itself.
- **Pulling the brake early.** Whoever is still below dies and comes back changed:
  - Oni, Vespera and Archangel return **undead**. They hit harder and ignore poison, doom and bleed.
  - The Golem and Sak are **rebuilt** by CURATOR's machines. They are armoured, gain HP, and ignore sleep, stun and slow.
  - Morwen, Elowen and Kitsune become **fragment-bound**. Their magic and MP rise and they resist fire.
- **The Bound.** If anyone was lost, the five become the Bound, a second party that starts in the Rootwell under the Conduit.
  - Their formation is locked and nobody else can join them.
  - Switch between Raven's company and the Bound from the menu, at a save point or on foot on the world map.
  - When a bound hero's reunion chapter comes up, the scene still plays, but the hero goes back to the Bound.
  - The Bound merge back into the whole party when the first ancient dragon falls.
- **Wait too long** and the cable snaps. You retry from the lift.
### CH13: After the Bell
Raven wakes in a refugee harbor assembled from cargo pontoons. Aurex is treating exhaustion with ordinary care; her visions are fragmented and unreliable. The player helps distribute lamp fuel, repairs a simple path and hears concrete news of the missing companions. This is a playable recovery scene, not a long cinematic that withholds control.
*Turning point:* Raven asks whether the world would be safer without him. Aurex refuses the premise that his body is a public utility. Ilyr admits that he heard other fragments take their hosts' voices during the activation. Their shared objective becomes freeing both groups without making either disposable.
### CH14: Roads Grow Teeth
Elowen is guiding survivors across the flooded Rootward. Her old maps are useful but incomplete. The player reconnects visible safe routes and defeats the Ash-Tide Warden that is diverting the current. No random terrain generation is allowed to make the rescue route unreachable.
*Turning point:* Elowen tries to keep the only good map to ensure nobody takes a dangerous path. Mara points out that nobody can wait for one guide forever. Elowen gives copies to the camp and rejoins the crew, beginning rather than completing her personal resolution.
### CH15: Light in the Flood
Morwen is holding an improvised barrier over a flooded gallery. She believes lowering it means admitting failure. The party clears the upper route, moves the residents, and then lets her release the spell. Her rescue is a puzzle and dialogue encounter, not another large boss that interrupts the recovery rhythm.
*Turning point:* For the first time she watches other people solve a problem while she rests. The lights go out; the evacuees turn on ordinary lanterns. Morwen joins the party and asks the Golem to show her a machine that works without a person pretending to be its fuel.
### CH16: The Engine That Waited
the Golem has found an unfinished rescue vessel, Wayfarer, designed before heartglass made efficiency politically irresistible. Its engine is heavier and slower, but its parts can be repaired. The party powers the old cooling channels, clears the assembly space and obtains the Iron Tortoise's voluntary help stabilizing the hull.
*Turning point:* Pell asks the Golem to leave a complete maintenance manual before he takes the ship. The manual becomes a visible object on the workshop bench. At the launch, the camera shows workers performing the procedure rather than the Golem making a last-second heroic adjustment.
### CH17: Wings Without Chains
Vespera is holding a damaged mooring while another crew tries to evacuate a cliff settlement. His proposed solution is to cut himself loose with the load. The player instead installs three reachable ballast anchors, using the dungeon's earlier rules in a changed topology. There is no hidden countdown forcing a sacrifice.
*Turning point:* Vespera must step away from the last lever and trust somebody else to hold it. Captain Edda refuses to put his name on the memorial and offers him a shift on the rescue roster instead. He returns to the airship without a grand speech.
### CH18: Names for the Living
Sak has been protecting the lower registry from Voss's remnants. Refugees need it intact to recover their identities, but it also contains the evidence of his forgeries. The party retrieves the original ledgers through changed canal routes and lets the residents, not Sak, choose how to record their names.
*Turning point:* Jori returns the satchel Sak used to carry false papers. It now holds blank forms and a proper seal issued by the refugee assembly. Sak joins with no claim that good intentions erased the harm; his full restitution remains an optional personal quest.
### CH19: The Hand That Wrote the Seal
Oni is sheltering people whose bindings she cannot safely remove alone. She has already submitted her name and testimony to Nacre. The party opens the cells one by one using their established consent-key rules, without requiring Oni to remain in the active combat party for a movement ability.
*Turning point:* A survivor refuses to thank Oni. The scene lets that refusal stand. Oni asks whether she can help prevent Voss from restoring the network before returning to face the hearing. The survivors grant a limited agreement and keep a copy of her account.
### CH20: The Ash Accord
The communities compare the local release procedures established in CH10 with what they learned during the reunions. the Golem supplies a distributed power circuit, Aurex supplies a consent check, and the returned companions supply working regional anchors. The game does not add three new ingredients after the player has already solved the regional problems.
*Turning point:* Rook arrives alive under escort. He provides the hidden route diagrams and admits that his activation imposed freedom as a command. He does not join the party, receive instant forgiveness or die conveniently before answering for the catastrophe. The assembly records both extraction and awakening as harms that require accountability.
### CH21: An Honest Kind of Power
This chapter is an optional preparation window rather than a mandatory checklist. The ship's crew room contains specific rumors pointing to the eight personal quests, the winter island, the starless reef, the capital echo and the silent singer. Every lead has an in-world source and a journal entry; no giant checklist obscures the world map.
*Turning point:* Completing each personal quest gives its character an ultimate weapon and final technique, and adds a scene to the ending. The base ending still resolves the main conflict when none are completed. Raven's personal quest is optional too; the essential body-consent decision was already made in the main story.
### CH22: A Gate with Two Keys
Wayfarer docks at Crown Heart. The player splits all eight heroes into two teams of four. The west route releases physical pressure while the east route removes the name-binding circuit. Both teams use the same inventory and progression rules; a formation safety check supplies guidance and a shared recovery point without requiring particular characters on either route.
*Turning point:* Voss has fused himself with the Crown Vessel to turn every interrupted relay into a single obedient network. Three telegraphed phases re-examine protection, coercion and shared action. The allies and communities perform the prepared release procedure while the party defeats the apparatus. Ilyr is offered a separate restored form; he chooses to remain with Raven for now because their agreement, unlike the old prison, can be ended by either of them.
### CH23: Let the Names Remain
The player walks through Hearthward after the victory. People use the restored records, the distributed engine and the returned rescue routes. Rook's hearing and Oni's promised appearance proceed; the ending does not equate accountability with a spectacle of punishment. Families remember those lost regardless of how many optional quests were completed.
*Turning point:* Each character receives a base epilogue and an enhanced one when their personal quest is finished. The additional scenes deepen the result rather than reveal that the ordinary ending was secretly false. Raven leaves his crown badge at a public memorial, then takes a maintenance shift aboard Wayfarer.
### CH24: The First Unborrowed Morning
Dawn reaches the rebuilt harbor. A lamp fails; two ordinary workers replace its part without calling for a chosen savior. Raven and Ilyr hear the same bell and name it differently. The last playable interaction is to step aboard the ship, not to choose which species deserves to exist.
*Turning point:* The title theme returns with its unresolved suspension finally completed. Credits include original-asset provenance and third-party notices. A post-credits image shows an unlit relay being repurposed as a public garden, with no new villain teaser required to understand the ending.

## Questlines

The 12 canon questlines Q01-Q12 are listed in the journal. The expansion adds 24 more: a personal questline for every hero, plus regional and Deep questlines. Each entry gives where it starts, what to do, and the reward.
### Q01: A Shield Nobody Asked For
- **Start:** Brackenford. Mara asks Raven to help identify the workers erased from the quarry ledger
- **Steps:** Recover three named records from the surface memorial and safe salvage route; speak to the families; let Mara choose the inscription
- **Choice:** Raven tries to add his confession to the memorial. Mara gives him a separate place to record it: the workers' names are not a stage for his redemption
- **Reward:** Open Hand; W006, S008
### Q02: A Lantern Left Burning
- **Start:** Bellharbor. A former apprentice asks Morwen to teach a technique that will not exhaust its user
- **Steps:** Collect observations at three inhabited lantern stations; test a low-power circuit with the apprentice; return to the flooded gallery
- **Choice:** Morwen must leave the apprentice alone to solve the last fault rather than taking over
- **Reward:** Ash Without Fire; W012, S016
### Q03: The Returners' Wall
- **Start:** High Aerie. Edda wants a record of rescues, not only deaths
- **Steps:** Find a damaged flight recorder; interview a survivor; repair a landing marker with the local crew
- **Choice:** Vespera reads the unedited account of the cable he cut and does not demand that it be balanced by his later deeds
- **Reward:** Unbound Descent; W018, S024
### Q04: The Unborrowed Engine
- **Start:** Cinderwake. Pell says the new engine works only while the Golem is standing beside it
- **Steps:** Have three workers follow the manual; identify failures in the instructions; revise and demonstrate a cold restart
- **Choice:** The final restart occurs with the Golem observing rather than operating
- **Reward:** Unborrowed Engine; W024, S032
### Q05: Every Road a Promise
- **Start:** Brackenford. Two displaced communities ask for contradictory route priorities
- **Steps:** Survey three visible crossings; uncover the route Elowen once concealed; construct a shared schedule through dialogue and a small switch puzzle
- **Choice:** Elowen admits her earlier bargain without asking the betrayed group to comfort her
- **Reward:** Many Paths; W030, S040
### Q06: The Right to Refuse
- **Start:** Nacre. A patient rejects a vision-based treatment order bearing Aurex's signature
- **Steps:** Read the original authorization; hear the patient's account; dismantle the automatic prognostic seal
- **Choice:** Aurex gives the patient the final decision even when she fears the outcome
- **Reward:** Borrowed Dawn; W036, S048
### Q07: What the Hand Remembers
- **Start:** Nacre. A survivor requests the location of another binding site, not Oni's apology
- **Steps:** Inspect three seals with their occupants; record the original design; remove the repeat-command mechanism
- **Choice:** Oni appears at the hearing without her sword and answers a specific question without qualification
- **Reward:** Unwritten Law; W042, S056
### Q08: The Names We Keep
- **Start:** Veyr. Jori brings people whose recovered legal names are not the ones they now choose
- **Steps:** Match ledgers with testimony; offer a chosen-name correction; recover an official seal from a safe puzzle chamber
- **Choice:** Sak accepts that a technically accurate forgery can still make decisions for others
- **Reward:** False Crown; W048, S064
### Q09: The Winter Without Sleep
- **Start:** Nacre. An island lantern burns each night while nobody in its village wakes
- **Steps:** Enter the Cradle; warm the shelters; defeat B13 after learning its lantern tell; speak to the Winter Hind
- **Choice:** The creature has been keeping the village in a dream to protect it from a winter that has already ended
- **Boss:** Lantern Eater (Lv 35). Relight the dark lantern with fire to weaken its choice.
- **Reward:** V07
### Q10: The Sea That Keeps Its Dead
- **Start:** Hearthward. A harbor bell repeats a missing ship's departure pattern
- **Steps:** Follow redundant sound-and-light beacons; restore the passenger register; defeat B14; accept V08's offer
- **Choice:** The Leviathan has been forced to repeat the wreck's last instant. The party ends the repetition without erasing the record
- **Boss:** Heartless Leviathan (Lv 38). Beacons flash before the tide returns to the front row.
- **Reward:** V08
### Q11: No More Crowns
- **Start:** Veyr. A recovered ministry automaton continues issuing Voss's orders under the capital
- **Steps:** Open the post-state echo chamber; read the command ledger; defeat B15 without obeying its false reward prompts
- **Choice:** An officer offers to keep the machine for benevolent use. The party disables its authority function and preserves the evidence
- **Boss:** Regent's Echo (Lv 36). The screen names the edict - Defend or dispel.
- **Reward:** A023
### Q12: A Song Nobody Owns
- **Start:** Nacre. The listening pool contains a voice excluded from both human and dragon testimony
- **Steps:** Obtain the listening phrase in Nacre; reach the reversible final antechamber; defeat B16 using its visible beat windows
- **Choice:** The Null Cantor asks only to be heard without being turned into a weapon
- **Boss:** Null Cantor (Lv 40). Defend through silence; act through response.
- **Reward:** A024
### QW1: Refuge Rock
- **Start:** Refuge Rock. Warden Marit Oakes of Refuge Rock: the spring is failing and four fishers (Ansa, Dov, Tamm and the girl Wynn) have not come back from the Tide Shelf wreck.
- **Steps:** Open the shelf gate; save Ansa from the wreck crabs; splint Dov's leg on the north rocks; find Tamm and Wynn in the Crown supply hulk, sheltered by Sergeant Kell's deserters; survive the harpy ambush on the way home.
- **Choice:** At the council: let Kell's nine Crown deserters onto the Rock (qw1_kell_in) or keep them on the shelf with a share of the rations (qw1_kell_out).
- **Reward:** A117 Lantern Charm, I003 x2, 2000 crowns
### QW6: The Last Beacon
- **Start:** The Last Beacon. Signalwoman Hesk: the Last Beacon, master lamp of the old keepers' signal network, went dark in the fault; its keeper died on the cliff with the key.
- **Steps:** Take Old Mattock's key from his cairn on the west ledge; climb to the lamp gallery and drive out the griffon roost; fill the empty fire-cup.
- **Choice:** Burn the Ministry's heartglass shard found in the lamp housing (qw6_heartglass) or burn the camp's winter oil and bury the heartglass under Mattock's cairn (qw6_oil).
- **Reward:** Grapnel Keel (land anywhere), A118 Driftglass Ring, 3000 crowns
### QC17: The Highway That Was
- **Start:** The Tessellate. At the Tessellate the Night Rider finds Mile 0 of the Ring Highway he still patrols; the tower stands on its old interchange.
- **Steps:** Surface half (P02): read the three final-night traffic logs in Traffic Control; reach the master dispatch console at the crown of the tower, past the Tessellate Engine; defeat the patrol drones. Deep half (U22): follow the highway's lower deck into the Lattice.
- **Choice:** At the dispatch console: end the patrol - unit relieved (qc17_ended) - or continue it on a new route, the ruin's broken roads (qc17_route).
- **Boss:** The Tessellate Engine (Lv 38). Hit the core; Defend on demolition.
- **Reward:** Surface half: A111 Windcord Knot. Deep half: see U22.
### QW7: Old Bellharbor
- **Start:** Tidewrack. After the fault, Wenna Coyle of Tidewrack hears that Keeper Ossery at Belltower Point has been hearing knocking through the lamp-glass at low tide: people alive under the sea in drowned Old Bellharbor
- **Steps:** Speak to Keeper Ossery; recover the smugglers' sea-glass helmet from the Brine Stair's contraband hold; fit it to the lighthouse's old diving bell; descend the sea-stair into Old Bellharbor
- **Choice:** Eleven people are trapped in the drowned customs vault and the bell carries six a trip: choose who goes first, or ask them to decide among themselves
- **Reward:** Diving Hull (flag lanternwake_diving); A118 Driftglass Ring; 2000 crowns
### QW2: The Ridge Road
- **Start:** Windrest. After the fault, Windrest's stablemaster Maddoc Harl asks for help: his daughter Ysolde took the flock up the ridge the morning the cliff road fell, and has not come down
- **Steps:** Climb the broken ridge road above Windrest; reach the stranded shepherds' camp; bring them down
- **Choice:** Carry the injured shepherd down the short way at once and leave the flock, or follow Old Crook, the ridgehorn ram, down the goat paths with everyone and every animal
- **Reward:** Ridgehorn (flag brackhorn_ridgehorn); A111 Windcord Knot
### QC10: The Host That Stopped Answering
- **Start:** Seraphel, Fallen Host. In the ruins of Seraphel, the Archangel Commander recognises his own host, which stopped answering him thirty years ago, and its last standing order: hold the sky until relieved
- **Steps:** Read the host's muster roll; find the sentinels still holding their posts; reach the choir loft where the choirmaster conducts a song nobody sings
- **Choice:** Order the sentinels to stand down, or tell them what he has seen below and let each of them choose
- **Boss:** The Hollow Seraph (Lv 38). Shadow; stop its hymn; Defend on the chord.
- **Reward:** W004 Witness Edge; A020 Mercy Thread
### QC09: The Court of Masks
- **Start:** Akagane, the Fox Court. Old Kiku, Kitsune's nursemaid, tells her that Tsubaki - the handmaid who wore the Empress's face for the court after the exile - is to be tried by the Nine-Tailed Magistrate at the Thousand Gates
- **Steps:** Seek an audience with Regent Shirogane in the Court of Masks; take the summons to the Thousand Gates; find the true gate through the illusion maze; stand for Tsubaki in the Magistrate's court; return to Kiku
- **Choice:** Before the court, Kitsune goes masked (as the court expects) or barefaced (as she is); afterwards she either takes the throne back unmasked or names Tsubaki regent and leaves the court its own face
- **Boss:** The Nine-Tailed Magistrate (Lv 30). Water douses foxfire; protect the marked one.
- **Reward:** Mirrorbrand (W040), Open-Eye Thread (A012) from the Mirror Walk
### QC12: The Cure That Was Sold
- **Start:** Harrowfen. In burned Harrowfen, Corvus's old apprentice Idony says the Weeping Grey has come back with the smoke, and the only stock of the Mirewold Remedy sits in the Leech Cathedral under a Crown licence
- **Steps:** Find the licence in the Board of Quarantine's ruined ledger; go to the Leech Cathedral; face Abbot Lamprey in the reliquary (or, if he fled into the leech-well before the fault, below it); bring the formula back to Idony
- **Choice:** Publish the formula to every herbalist in the Mirewold, or leave it with Idony's physic house to brew safely and teach from
- **Boss:** Abbot Lamprey (Lv 25). Fire and light; cure poison after the censer.
- **Reward:** Warm Lantern (A015); Salt Locket (A013) from the physic house
### QC13: A Court Too Long in Mourning
- **Start:** Coldharbour Citadel. At Coldharbour's causeway the Lich King stops: the Frosthorn Warden still keeps his seat shut, and his court still waits inside
- **Steps:** Pass the Frosthorn Warden; enter the sealed inner court; speak with Chancellor Vesk, Captain Ormund and Ysolde; learn that most of the court went down the Lich Stair into the Deep; follow them below (U35)
- **Choice:** Release the three who stayed at Coldharbour, or ask them to keep the seat until the rest come home
- **Boss:** The Frosthorn Warden (Lv 35). Fire, then light; cure silence.
- **Reward:** continues at U35 The Lich Stair
### QW3: Harrowfen Burning
- **Start:** Harrowfen. Mother Brannock, at the burned Plague Gate, says the Board's cleansing order is still being carried out in the Ward - by one warden-captain who will not stop
- **Steps:** Enter the Burning Ward; find three trapped families; open the lazar-house; face Warden-Captain Ostrey
- **Choice:** Hand Ostrey to the survivors' judgment, or let him carry the sick out with his own hands
- **Reward:** Grand Tonic x2, 1500 crowns, Elixir
### QW4: The Mire Ferry
- **Start:** Sickle Hamlet. The fault drowned Sickle Hamlet's causeway and sank Old Pike's ferry; Reeve Holloway wants to teach the party's brackhorn to wade the mire on reed pattens
- **Steps:** Cut three bundles of ironreed on the Drowned Causeway; find Old Pike on his islet; drive off the mire-beast that nests in the sunken road; bring the reed to Granny Sedge for the pattens
- **Choice:** Give Pike the first pair of pattens (he guides wading riders now) or rebuild his punt from the spare reed

## Post-game

- **After the ending**, choose *Continue* to return to the world before the Final Descent, with your clear recorded.
- **The Belfry Below (QEPI).** Talk to old bellwright Anse beside the bell in Hearthward's market; he lowers you in his diving bell to drowned Veyr.
  - **The floors:** the Drowned Nave, the Bellrope Galleries, the Carillon and the Belfry.
  - **Enemies:** about level 84-92.
  - **The Carillon puzzle:** ring its three bells in order, FIRST, SECOND, then the cracked LAST - AND RIGHT, to open the stair.
  - **Boss: the Bell That Rang Wrong (Lv 92).** Earth dulls it, cure doom quickly, and Defend when every bell in the city answers (the Knell).
  - **Rewards:** The Right Bell, Veyr's Clapper, the Carillon Mantle and Nave-Warden Plate.
  - **Afterwards:** a final epilogue plays at Hearthward.
- **Completion.** Records > Stats shows overall completion as the average of places visited, questlines, chests, Vestiges, superbosses and the bestiary. 100% unlocks *Every Map Filled*.
- **New Game+.** From the ending menu. It carries levels, gear and the bestiary.
